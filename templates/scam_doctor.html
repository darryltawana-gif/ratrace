What Scam Doctor looks for

Fake payment screenshotsForged mobile-money or bank alerts, “I have paid”, overpayment claims.
Advance-fee / overpaymentPay a fee, refund the difference, customs or delivery fee, prize tax.
Phishing & impersonationFake login links, fake delivery links, someone pretending to be a bank or seller.
Romance / relationshipBuilding trust quickly, then asking for money, airtime or gifts.
Fake jobs / investmentPay-to-work, guaranteed returns, crypto or doubling-money offers.
Fake delivery / courierFake parcel tracking, customs clearance, courier payment requests.

How it works

  1. Rules first. Links, payment claims, urgency, secrecy, Twi and Pidgin phrases are checked instantly and free.
  2. AI second. When the safety team has configured a key, a text/vision model reads the message or screenshot for a second opinion. That content is sent to the configured AI provider only when AI is on.
  3. You decide. You get a risk level, the exact warning signs and practical advice. Scam Doctor never publicly accuses anyone.
  4. Safety team review. The scan is stored in the database so the creators can review it and improve the protection.
Scam Doctor cannot see the live Quality Proof video. It only checks what you paste or upload. A “safe” result is not a guarantee — always verify payments in your own bank or mobile-money app.
